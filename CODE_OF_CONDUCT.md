# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participants in this project are expected to engage respectfully and in good
faith. Disagreement about technical direction is normal and welcome;
disrespect, harassment, or personal attacks are not.

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for users of the project
- Showing empathy toward other contributors

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, public or private
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews

## Scope

This applies within all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions)
and when representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainers by
opening a private security advisory or contacting a maintainer directly
through their GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed and addressed
proportionally, up to and including a temporary or permanent ban from
project spaces.

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards of acceptable behavior
and will take appropriate, fair corrective action in response to violations.
